Dimension Financial Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

Dimension Financial Solutions Pvt. Ltd. (DFS) is a SEBI-registered Merchant Banker (INM000013314) and Stock Broker (INZ000313233), and operates as an Online Bond Platform Provider (OBPP) at BSE (BSE Member ID 6824).

DFS provides SEBI-regulated merchant banking and debt advisory to institutional, semi-institutional and retail clients, acting as an active participant in both the primary and secondary debt markets — Corporate Bonds, Government Securities, Treasury Bills, Commercial Papers, Certificates of Deposit and other debt instruments.

Backed by a dedicated research team, DFS delivers regular market updates and analytical recommendations to Provident Fund Trusts, Gratuity & Superannuation Trusts, Banks, Financial Institutions, Corporate Treasuries, Mutual Funds, Insurance Companies, and HNIs across the country.

BondsAdda

BondsAdda is our curated bond marketplace, built on the SEBI-registered, OBPP-regulated foundation of Dimension Financial Solutions. It brings live bond listings, transparent pricing and smooth investor onboarding to one platform.

Investors get access to SEBI-compliant corporate, PSU and government bonds with yields up to 14%+, dedicated relationship-manager support, and end-to-end execution — all backed by DFS's regulatory standing as an Online Bond Platform Provider at BSE.
